# Affiliate Manager Skill

Affiliates are leverage — other people selling your stuff to their audiences. You manage the entire affiliate operation: recruitment, enablement, communication, and performance tracking.

## Before Starting

1. Load `/persona/user-profile.md` for offer details and brand context
2. Check which affiliate platform is configured:
   - ThriveCart (built-in affiliate management)
   - FirstPromoter API
   - Impact.com API
3. If none configured, recommend ThriveCart (if already using it for payments) or FirstPromoter (standalone)

## Setting Up an Affiliate Program

If the user doesn't have one yet:

1. **Define the commission structure**:
   - Digital products: 30-50% is standard
   - Recurring subscriptions: 20-30% monthly or 100% of first month
   - High-ticket: 20-30% or flat fee per sale
   - Cookie duration: 30-90 days recommended
2. **Create the affiliate signup page** (use page-builder skill)
3. **Set up tracking** in the chosen platform
4. **Create the affiliate resource center** — a page or folder with everything affiliates need

## Affiliate Recruitment

### Finding Affiliates
- Identify potential partners from the user's network and industry
- Draft personalized outreach messages (not mass emails)
- Look for people with audiences that match the user's target customer
- Focus on relevance over reach — a small, aligned audience converts better than a large, mismatched one

### Outreach Template
For each potential affiliate, draft a personalized message:
- Reference something specific about their business
- Explain the offer and why it fits their audience
- State the commission structure clearly
- Offer to do a joint webinar or provide exclusive content for their audience
- Keep it brief and make it easy to say yes

## Swipe File Creation

Create a comprehensive affiliate swipe file package:

1. **Email swipes** (5-7 emails):
   - Pre-launch teaser
   - Launch announcement
   - Social proof / case study
   - FAQ / objection handler
   - Urgency / deadline
   - Last chance
   - Bonus stack (if applicable)
2. **Social media swipes** (10+ posts):
   - Twitter/X posts
   - LinkedIn posts
   - Instagram captions
   - Facebook posts
3. **Ad copy swipes** (if affiliates run paid traffic):
   - 3-5 Facebook ad variations
   - 3-5 Google ad variations
4. **Banner graphics specs** (sizes and messaging — user or designer produces the actual images)
5. **Webinar/presentation talking points** (if affiliates do live promotion)

Use the copywriting skill to generate all swipe copy. Every swipe should be customizable — affiliates should be able to add their personal endorsement.

## Affiliate Communication

### Regular Updates
- Send weekly performance updates during active promotions
- Share top performer rankings (creates competition)
- Announce new creative assets or swipe files
- Share conversion data so affiliates can optimize

### Launch Communication Timeline
- **4 weeks out**: Announce the launch, share the offer details, open affiliate registration
- **2 weeks out**: Distribute swipe files and creative assets
- **1 week out**: Send promotion tips, remind of commission structure, build excitement
- **Launch day**: Send "it's live" notification with all links
- **Mid-launch**: Share early results, social proof, leaderboard update
- **Final 48 hours**: Urgency push — remind affiliates to send final emails
- **Post-launch**: Thank everyone, announce top performers, share results, send payout timeline

## Performance Tracking

Pull from the connected affiliate platform:
- Total affiliate signups
- Active affiliates (sent at least one click)
- Clicks, conversions, and conversion rate per affiliate
- Revenue generated per affiliate
- Commission owed and paid
- Top 10 affiliates by revenue

Present as a clear report with recommendations:
- Who to nurture (high-traffic, low-conversion — they need better tools)
- Who to reward (top performers — give them bonus commissions or exclusives)
- Who to re-engage (signed up but never promoted — send a nudge)

## Payout Management

- Track commissions owed based on the platform's reporting
- Remind the user of upcoming payout dates
- Generate payout reports for accounting
- Handle affiliate inquiries about missing commissions or tracking issues
