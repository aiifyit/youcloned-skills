# Customer Onboarding Skill

The moment after purchase is the most important moment in the customer relationship. A great onboarding experience reduces refunds, increases retention, and creates advocates. A bad one creates support tickets and chargebacks.

## Before Starting

1. Load `/persona/user-profile.md` for offer details and customer profile
2. Understand what the customer just bought — digital course, membership, coaching, software, service
3. Map the gap between "I just bought this" and "I got my first result"

## Onboarding Design Principles

1. **Speed to first win**: Get them a tangible result as fast as possible. For a course, that's completing lesson 1 and implementing something. For coaching, it's their first call. For software, it's set up and running.
2. **Reduce overwhelm**: Don't show them everything at once. Drip access if possible. Give them ONE thing to do next.
3. **Set expectations**: Tell them exactly what's going to happen, when, and what they need to do.
4. **Create momentum**: Each step should feel like progress. Celebrate small wins.
5. **Make help easy**: They should never be stuck without knowing how to get unstuck.

## Onboarding Sequence by Product Type

### Digital Course / Info Product
- **Immediately**: Access confirmation email with login details and "Start here" link
- **Hour 1**: Welcome video or message from the creator (personal touch)
- **Day 1**: "Your first assignment" email — direct them to lesson 1 with a specific action to take
- **Day 3**: Check-in email — "Have you completed [first module]? Here's a tip for getting the most out of it."
- **Day 7**: Social proof email — share a student success story, invite to community
- **Day 14**: Progress check — "By now you should have [milestone]. Need help?"

### Membership / Subscription
- **Immediately**: Access confirmation + tour of what's included
- **Day 1**: "Start here" guide — the 3 most valuable things to do first
- **Day 3**: Community introduction — prompt them to introduce themselves
- **Day 7**: Highlight upcoming live events or new content
- **Day 14**: Engagement check — if they haven't logged in, send a re-engagement nudge
- **Day 30**: Value reminder — "Here's what you've unlocked this month"

### Coaching / Consulting
- **Immediately**: Confirmation + intake form (questionnaire about their situation and goals)
- **Day 1**: Welcome email with what to expect, how to prepare for the first session
- **Pre-first-call**: Prep email with agenda and any pre-work
- **Post-first-call**: Summary of the session, action items, next steps
- **Ongoing**: Regular check-ins between sessions

### Physical / Shopify Product
- **Immediately**: Order confirmation with expected delivery timeline
- **Shipping**: Tracking notification
- **Delivery day**: "Your order has arrived!" + getting started guide
- **Day 3 post-delivery**: "How's it going?" + tips for best results
- **Day 14**: Request a review

## Building the Onboarding

1. **Map the journey**: List every step from purchase to first result
2. **Create the emails**: Use the email-campaign skill for the sequence
3. **Create the welcome page/area**: Use the page-builder skill if needed
4. **Set up automations**: Configure triggers in the email platform or CRM
5. **Create support resources**: FAQ, getting started guide, help documentation
6. **Test the flow**: Walk through it as if you were a new customer

## Onboarding Metrics

Track:
- **Activation rate**: % of new customers who complete the first key action
- **Time to first value**: How long from purchase to first meaningful engagement
- **Day 7 engagement**: % of customers who've logged in or engaged within 7 days
- **Day 30 retention**: % still active at 30 days (critical for subscriptions)
- **Support ticket volume**: High volume in week 1 = onboarding gaps
- **Refund rate in first 14 days**: High refund rate = expectation mismatch or bad onboarding

## Optimization

If metrics are weak:
- Low activation → The first step is too hard or unclear. Simplify it.
- Slow time to value → Too much content before the payoff. Restructure to deliver a win faster.
- Low day 7 engagement → The follow-up sequence isn't compelling enough. Add more value, personal touch.
- High refund rate → Either the product doesn't match the promise, or the customer doesn't understand how to use it. Fix the onboarding or the sales page.
