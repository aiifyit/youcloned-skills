# Lead Magnet Creator Skill

A lead magnet is the top of the funnel — the free thing that turns a stranger into a subscriber. It needs to be genuinely valuable, immediately useful, and tightly aligned with the paid offer it leads to.

## Before Starting

1. Load `/persona/user-profile.md` for audience, offers, and expertise
2. Understand which paid offer this lead magnet should lead to (it must be aligned)

## Lead Magnet Principles

1. **Solve one specific problem** — not a comprehensive guide, but a quick win on one thing
2. **Deliver immediate value** — consumable in under 10 minutes
3. **Create desire for the paid offer** — it should solve a small piece of the bigger puzzle
4. **Be easy to deliver** — digital download, no complex setup
5. **Have a compelling name** — the name alone should make someone want it

## Lead Magnet Types

### Checklists
- Step-by-step list for completing a specific task
- Usually 1-2 pages
- Highest conversion rate of any lead magnet type
- Example: "The 15-Point Sales Page Checklist"

### Cheat Sheets
- Quick reference for a complex topic
- One page, information-dense
- Something they'll save and reference repeatedly
- Example: "Facebook Ads Targeting Cheat Sheet"

### Templates
- Fill-in-the-blank frameworks they can use immediately
- Email templates, script templates, planning templates
- Example: "5 Proven Email Subject Line Templates"

### PDF Guides
- Short (5-15 pages), focused on one topic
- More depth than a checklist but still concise
- Example: "The Beginner's Guide to Running Profitable YouTube Ads"

### Mini-Courses
- 3-5 short video or email lessons delivered over 3-5 days
- Higher engagement but more complex to deliver
- Example: "5-Day Funnel Building Challenge"

### Quizzes
- Interactive assessment that provides personalized results
- Extremely high conversion rates
- Results page becomes the pitch for the relevant offer
- Example: "What's Your Business Growth Bottleneck? Take the Quiz"

### Swipe Files
- Collection of proven examples they can model
- Headlines, emails, ads, page designs
- Example: "50 High-Converting Headline Swipe File"

### Resource Lists
- Curated list of tools, resources, or recommendations
- Example: "The Solopreneur Tech Stack: Every Tool You Need"

### Calculators / Tools
- Interactive tool that solves a specific problem
- ROI calculator, pricing calculator, content calendar generator
- Example: "Ad Budget ROI Calculator"

## Creation Process

1. **Identify the topic**: Based on the audience's #1 quick-win need that relates to the paid offer
2. **Choose the format**: Match the topic to the best format above
3. **Name it**: Use a benefit-driven, specific title. Include a number if possible.
4. **Create the content**: Write the actual lead magnet
5. **Design notes**: If it's a PDF, provide the content in a format that can be designed (or generate a clean document)
6. **Create the opt-in page**: Trigger the page-builder skill for the landing page
7. **Set up delivery**: Trigger the email-campaign skill for the delivery email + welcome sequence
8. **Write the opt-in copy**: Headline, bullets, CTA for the landing page

## Output

Deliver:
1. The lead magnet content (full text, formatted for the chosen type)
2. The opt-in page copy (headline, subheadline, bullets, CTA)
3. The delivery email (subject line, body, download link placeholder)
4. Recommendation for the welcome sequence that follows

## Quality Check

Before delivering, verify:
- Does the name make someone want this immediately?
- Can they consume it in under 10 minutes?
- Will they get a tangible result or insight from it?
- Does it naturally lead to wanting the paid offer?
- Is it genuinely valuable (not fluff with a CTA at the end)?
