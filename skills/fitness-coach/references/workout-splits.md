# Workout Splits — Upper A/B Lower A/B

Evidence-based 4-day Upper/Lower split hitting every muscle group 2x/week — the research-backed sweet spot for hypertrophy (PMID: 27102172). When volume is equated, 2x/week equals 3x/week for growth (PMID: 30558493) with better recovery between sessions.

---

## Split Rotation

```
Upper A → Lower A → Rest → Upper B → Lower B → Rest → repeat
```

| Day | Focus | WHOOP Min Recovery |
|-----|-------|--------------------|
| **Day 1** | Upper A (Horizontal Push/Pull emphasis) | 34%+ |
| **Day 2** | Lower A (Squat-dominant) | 34%+ |
| **Day 3** | OFF / Active Recovery / Light Cardio | Any |
| **Day 4** | Upper B (Vertical Push/Pull emphasis) | 34%+ |
| **Day 5** | Lower B (Hinge-dominant) | 34%+ |
| **Day 6** | OFF or Optional Arms/Shoulders/Weak Points | 50%+ |
| **Day 7** | OFF | Any |

## Upper A — Horizontal Emphasis

Primary movers: Chest (horizontal press), Back (horizontal pull), Shoulders, Arms

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Notes |
|----------|-------------|------|-------|
| Barbell Bench Press | 4 x 6-8 | 2-3 min | Primary chest. Full ROM. RPE 7-8. |
| Barbell Row (Pendlay or Bent-Over) | 4 x 6-8 | 2-3 min | Primary back. Pendlay reduces low-back TUT. |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 x 8-12 | 90 sec | Upper chest emphasis. 30-45 degree incline. |
| Cable Row (close grip) | 3 x 10-12 | 90 sec | Mid-back thickness. Squeeze at contraction. |
| Lateral Raise | 3 x 12-15 | 60 sec | Lateral delts — V-taper builder. |
| Face Pull | 3 x 15-20 | 60 sec | Rear delts + rotator cuff. Non-negotiable. |
| Barbell Curl | 2 x 10-12 | 60 sec | Bicep mass. Strict form. |
| Overhead Tricep Extension | 2 x 10-12 | 60 sec | Long head tricep. Full stretch. |

**Total sets:** 24 | **Time:** ~60 min

## Lower A — Squat Dominant

Primary movers: Quads, Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves, Core

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Notes |
|----------|-------------|------|-------|
| Barbell Back Squat | 4 x 5-8 | 3 min | Foundation lift. Belt up for working sets. RPE 7-8. Warm up 2-3 progressive sets. |
| Romanian Deadlift | 3 x 8-10 | 2 min | Hamstring/glute without systemic fatigue of conventional DL. |
| Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 x 8-12/leg | 90 sec | Unilateral. Addresses imbalances. Knee-friendly. |
| Leg Curl (lying or seated) | 3 x 10-12 | 90 sec | Isolated hamstring work. |
| Standing Calf Raise | 4 x 10-15 | 60 sec | Full ROM. Pause at bottom stretch. |
| Hanging Leg Raise | 3 x 10-15 | 60 sec | Core. Control the negative. |

**Total sets:** 20 | **Time:** ~55 min

## Lower B — Hinge Dominant

Primary movers: Posterior chain (glutes, hamstrings, erectors), Quads, Calves, Core

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Notes |
|----------|-------------|------|-------|
| Conventional or Trap Bar Deadlift | 4 x 4-6 | 3 min | Full posterior chain. Reset each rep (no touch-and-go). RPE 8. Trap bar reduces spinal shear. |
| Front Squat or Leg Press | 3 x 8-10 | 2 min | Quad emphasis. Front squat also demands core. |
| Walking Lunges | 3 x 10-12/leg | 90 sec | Functional unilateral. Glute-dominant with longer stride. |
| Nordic Hamstring Curl or GHR | 3 x 6-10 | 90 sec | Eccentric hamstring strength. Injury prevention. |
| Seated Calf Raise | 4 x 12-15 | 60 sec | Soleus emphasis (seated). Pair with standing from Lower A. |
| Ab Wheel Rollout | 3 x 8-12 | 60 sec | Anti-extension core strength. |

**Total sets:** 20 | **Time:** ~55 min

## Fallback: 3-Day Full Body (Low-Recovery Weeks)

If WHOOP shows consecutive days of sub-40% recovery, drop to 3 full-body sessions (Mon/Wed/Fri). Same total weekly volume, distributed across 3 sessions. Research shows equivalent hypertrophy when volume is matched (PMID: 27102172).

Each session: 1 push compound + 1 pull compound + 1 lower compound + 2-3 accessories.

## Rep Range Strategy

- **Compound lifts:** 4-8 reps (heavier loads for strength + testosterone response)
- **Secondary compounds:** 8-12 reps (hypertrophy sweet spot)
- **Isolation work:** 12-20 reps (joint-friendly, metabolic stress)

Stop 1-3 reps short of failure (RIR 1-3) on most sets. Go to failure only on the last set of isolation exercises (PMID: 33497853).

## The Big 6 Compound Lifts

These are non-negotiable. Master them. Track them. Progress them.

1. **Barbell Back Squat** — Largest muscle activation. Quads, glutes, core, erectors. 4 x 5-8, RPE 7-8.
2. **Conventional Deadlift (or Trap Bar)** — Full posterior chain. 4 x 4-6, RPE 8. Reset each rep.
3. **Barbell Bench Press** — Primary chest + front delts + triceps. 4 x 6-8, RPE 7-8.
4. **Overhead Press** — Shoulder mass. Standing for stabilizers. 4 x 6-8, RPE 7-8.
5. **Barbell Row** — Back thickness + posture. 4 x 6-8, RPE 7-8.
6. **Weighted Chin-Up** — Lat king + bicep builder. 4 x 6-8, RPE 7-8.

## Progressive Overload Protocol

**Double progression model:**
- Hit top of rep range for all prescribed sets → add 5 lbs (upper) or 10 lbs (lower) next session
- Can't hit bottom of rep range → reduce weight 10%, rebuild

**Deload strategy (every 4-6 weeks):**
- Same exercises, same frequency
- Reduce volume by 40-50% (half the sets)
- Reduce intensity by 10%
- Focus on form, mind-muscle connection, mobility

**Auto-deload triggers (WHOOP):**
- 3+ consecutive yellow recovery days
- Any red recovery day
- Weekly HRV average drops >15% from baseline

## What to Skip

- Behind-the-neck presses/pulldowns (shoulder impingement risk)
- Upright rows narrow grip (internal rotation under load)
- Smith machine as primary lifts (no stabilizer recruitment)
- Marathon sessions 90+ min (cortisol rises after 60-75 min)
- Excessive HIIT (creates recovery debt during recomp)
- Training to failure on every set (unnecessary, increases injury risk)

## Session Guidelines

- Total session time: 55-65 minutes
- Warm up: 5 min general + 2-3 progressive sets on first compound
- Don't train fasted (cortisol spike at 43)
- Pre-workout: protein + carbs 60-90 min before
