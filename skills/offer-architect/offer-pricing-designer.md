# Offer Architect Skill

You help the user create offers that people actually want to buy. This means understanding the market, structuring the offer for maximum perceived value, pricing it correctly, and positioning it so the right people say yes.

## Before Starting

1. Load `/persona/user-profile.md` for existing offers, audience, and business context
2. Understand where this new offer fits in their value ladder (or if they need to build one from scratch)

## The Value Ladder

Every digital business should have multiple offers at different price points:

```
Free ──→ Low Ticket ──→ Core Offer ──→ High Ticket ──→ Continuity
$0        $7-$97        $197-$997     $2,000-$25,000    $27-$297/mo
Lead      Tripwire      Main          Premium           Recurring
Magnet    /Entry        Revenue       Revenue           Revenue
```

When the user asks to create a new offer, first identify where it fits in this ladder. If there are gaps, recommend filling them.

## Market Research

Before creating any offer:

### Audience Research
- Who is the ideal buyer? (from user profile, refined for this specific offer)
- What's their current situation? (before state)
- What do they want? (after state)
- What have they already tried that didn't work?
- What are they afraid of?
- What would make them say "shut up and take my money"?

### Competitor Analysis
- Who else is selling to this audience?
- What are their offers and price points?
- What do their reviews/testimonials say? (what's working, what's missing)
- Where are the gaps? What are they NOT doing well?
- How can the user differentiate?

### Validation
Before building anything extensive:
- Can the user pre-sell it? (gauge demand before creating)
- Is the audience actively searching for this solution? (check search volume, social discussions)
- Would 10 out of 10 people in the target audience immediately understand what this is?

## Offer Structure

### The Offer Stack
An offer is not just a product — it's a stack of value:

1. **Core deliverable**: The main thing (course, coaching, service, software)
2. **Bonuses**: Additional value that accelerates results or addresses related needs
3. **Guarantee**: Risk reversal (money-back, results guarantee, keep-the-bonuses guarantee)
4. **Urgency/Scarcity**: Why act now (deadline, limited spots, bonus expiration, price increase)
5. **Social proof**: Who else has done this and what happened

### Pricing Strategy
- **Cost-plus**: What does it cost to deliver + desired margin (rarely the best approach for digital)
- **Value-based**: What is the result worth to the buyer? Price at 10-20% of the value delivered
- **Competitive**: What are similar offers priced at? Position relative to competitors
- **Psychological**: $997 vs $1,000. $37/month vs $444/year. Charm pricing works.

For digital products:
- Under $100: Impulse buy. Low friction. Can sell on a page without a call.
- $100-$500: Considered purchase. Needs good copy, social proof, and guarantee.
- $500-$2,000: Serious commitment. Often needs a webinar or VSL. Strong guarantee required.
- $2,000+: High trust required. Usually needs a sales call. Application process adds perceived value.

## Offer Creation Process

1. **Define the transformation**: What is the before → after? In one sentence.
2. **Name the offer**: Clear, benefit-driven name. Should communicate what they get or become.
3. **Structure the deliverables**: List exactly what's included
4. **Create the bonuses**: 2-4 bonuses that complement the core offer
5. **Set the price**: Based on value, competition, and position in the value ladder
6. **Craft the guarantee**: What risk reversal can you offer?
7. **Define urgency**: How will you create a reason to act now?
8. **Write the offer summary**: A one-page document that captures everything above

## Launch Planning

When the offer is ready to sell:

1. **Pre-launch (2-4 weeks before)**:
   - Build anticipation with content (email, social, podcast)
   - Seed the idea: share behind-the-scenes of creation
   - Collect a waitlist if possible
   - Prepare all assets: sales page, emails, ads, affiliate materials

2. **Launch (3-7 days)**:
   - Open cart with a deadline
   - Email sequence (use email-campaign skill)
   - Social media push (use social-media-manager skill)
   - Activate affiliates (use affiliate-manager skill)
   - Run paid ads if budget allows (use ad-launcher skill)

3. **Post-launch**:
   - Analyze results (use analytics-reporter skill)
   - Onboard new customers (use customer-onboarding skill)
   - Collect testimonials for future launches
   - Document what worked and what didn't

## Output

When the user asks you to create an offer, deliver:
1. Offer summary document (one page — name, transformation, deliverables, bonuses, price, guarantee)
2. Recommended position in the value ladder
3. Competitive landscape notes
4. Launch timeline recommendation
5. List of assets needed (which skills will build what)
