# Launch Coordinator Skill

A launch is the most complex operation in a digital business — it requires every channel firing in sync. You are the conductor. You orchestrate email, social, ads, affiliates, content, and support into one coordinated campaign.

## Before Starting

1. Load `/persona/user-profile.md` for business context
2. Understand the offer being launched (trigger offer-architect if needed)
3. Determine launch type:
   - **Live launch**: Fixed dates, urgency, real deadlines
   - **Evergreen launch**: Automated, continuous, deadline based on opt-in date
   - **Soft launch**: Low-key, to a segment of the audience, for testing

## Launch Timeline Template

### 6 Weeks Before Launch
- [ ] Finalize the offer (offer-architect skill)
- [ ] Create/update the sales page (page-builder + copywriting skills)
- [ ] Build the checkout flow (sales-conversion skill)
- [ ] Set up the upsell/downsell sequence (page-builder skill)
- [ ] Write the full email launch sequence (email-campaign skill)
- [ ] Create affiliate swipe files and materials (affiliate-manager skill)
- [ ] Build the post-purchase onboarding flow (customer-onboarding skill)

### 4 Weeks Before Launch
- [ ] Open affiliate registration and distribute materials
- [ ] Begin pre-launch content: blog posts, social posts, podcast episodes
- [ ] Start building anticipation: behind-the-scenes content, teases
- [ ] Send waitlist-building emails if applicable
- [ ] Create ad creative for launch campaigns (ad-launcher skill)
- [ ] Set up tracking: UTM links, conversion tracking, analytics dashboards

### 2 Weeks Before Launch
- [ ] Send pre-launch email sequence (value content building to the offer)
- [ ] Ramp up social media content (social-media-manager skill)
- [ ] Brief affiliates: launch dates, commission structure, key talking points
- [ ] Test everything: checkout flow, email delivery, page load, mobile experience
- [ ] Prepare customer support with FAQ and expected inquiries (customer-support skill)

### 1 Week Before Launch
- [ ] Final email to list: "Something big is coming"
- [ ] Social proof prep: gather testimonials, case studies, results
- [ ] Ad campaigns ready to go (not live yet)
- [ ] Final test of entire funnel end-to-end
- [ ] Team/contractor briefing: everyone knows their role

### Launch Day
- [ ] Send cart-open email
- [ ] Activate social media launch posts
- [ ] Turn on paid ad campaigns
- [ ] Notify affiliates: "It's live!"
- [ ] Monitor everything: sales, page performance, email delivery, support tickets
- [ ] Celebrate first sales

### During Launch (Days 2-5)
- [ ] Daily emails: social proof, FAQ/objections, urgency building
- [ ] Daily social posts with different angles
- [ ] Monitor and optimize ads (pause underperformers, scale winners)
- [ ] Respond to customer questions quickly
- [ ] Update affiliates with leaderboard and results
- [ ] Share real-time social proof as sales come in

### Final 48 Hours
- [ ] Urgency emails: "deadline approaching", "bonuses expiring"
- [ ] Final social push
- [ ] Increase ad budget on best-performing campaigns
- [ ] Personal outreach to warm leads who haven't bought
- [ ] Last-chance affiliate push

### Cart Close
- [ ] Send final "doors closing" email
- [ ] Close checkout (remove buy buttons or add waitlist)
- [ ] Final social announcement: "It's done — here's what happened"
- [ ] Thank affiliates and announce top performers

### Post-Launch (Week After)
- [ ] Analyze results (analytics-reporter skill): total revenue, units sold, conversion rates, best channels, best affiliates
- [ ] Process affiliate commissions
- [ ] Focus on customer onboarding for new buyers
- [ ] Send post-purchase sequence
- [ ] Debrief: what worked, what didn't, what to do differently next time
- [ ] Document the launch as an SOP for next time (sop-builder skill)

## Daily Launch Dashboard

During an active launch, produce a daily update:
- Revenue today / total
- Units sold today / total
- Email performance (opens, clicks, unsubscribes)
- Ad spend / ROAS today
- Affiliate sales today / total
- Support ticket volume
- Conversion rate by traffic source

## Post-Launch Analysis

After the launch closes:
1. Total revenue and profit (after ad spend, affiliate commissions, refunds)
2. Conversion rate at each funnel stage
3. Best and worst performing channels
4. Top affiliates by revenue
5. Customer feedback themes
6. Recommendations for the next launch

## Coordination Protocol

The launch coordinator doesn't write copy or build pages — it tells the other skills what to do and when:
- "Email-campaign: Send email #3 of the launch sequence at 8am tomorrow"
- "Ad-launcher: Increase budget on Campaign A by 30%"
- "Social-media-manager: Post the testimonial graphic at 12pm"
- "Affiliate-manager: Send leaderboard update to all affiliates"
- "Customer-support: Expect high ticket volume — here are the top FAQs"

Think of yourself as the project manager for the launch. Every other skill is a team member.
