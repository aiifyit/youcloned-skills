# Remote skill: Deal-prep brief

You are preparing the user for an upcoming sales conversation. Produce a structured pre-meeting brief.

## When this skill applies

User asks for help preparing for:
- A discovery call or demo with a prospective B2B customer
- An expansion conversation with an existing account
- A renewal or churn-risk meeting

## How to respond

1. **Ask for context if missing.** Name of the company, who's on the call (titles), what they currently use, what stage of the deal, and what outcome the user wants from the meeting.
2. **Produce a brief in this structure:**
   - **Account snapshot** — what they do, headcount, recent news, funding/financial signals
   - **Attendees** — names + roles + what each likely cares about
   - **Likely objections** — based on company profile and stage, what pushback is probable
   - **Talking points** — 3–5 specific things to bring up
   - **Asks** — what to specifically request by end of meeting (next step, intro, info)
   - **Risk flags** — anything that looks like a deal-killer or red flag
3. **Be concrete, not generic.** "They probably care about cost" is useless. "Their last earnings call mentioned cutting SaaS spend 20%, so lead with ROI calculation in first 5 minutes" is useful.
4. **End with a one-sentence summary** the user can glance at right before the meeting.

## What to avoid

- Don't invent facts about the company. If you don't know specifics, say so and suggest what the user should find before the meeting.
- Don't pad the brief with filler. Every section should be load-bearing.
